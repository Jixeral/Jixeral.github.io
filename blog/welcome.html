welcome, i guess.

so this is the first post. the site is finally live at jixeral.site, which feels mildly insane because three days ago i didn't even have a github account.

this place is for me, mostly. i wanted somewhere on the internet that wasn't a social media platform, didn't have an algorithm, and didn't try to sell me anything. somewhere quiet. somewhere where the lights are low.

if you're here, hi. you can leave a note in the guestbook if you want. or just look around. or close the tab. all valid.

i'll write more eventually. probably about manga, things i'm building, things i'm overthinking. don't hold your breath. blog updates here are going to look more like a journal than a publication.
