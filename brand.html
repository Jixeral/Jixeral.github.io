— page 05 / the future label

a brand called
JIXERAL.

i want to design clothes. just designs, fully online — i upload, you order, a printer ships it, i never touch a box. that's the dream.

01 / the idea

punpun-coded.
melancholic.
not for everyone.

think: dark t-shirts with weird little drawings. quiet quotes. illustrations that mean something if you know. nothing aggressive. just soft, sad, technical, mine.

name: Jixeral  ·  vibe: manga-inspired streetwear  ·  status: still planning

02 / print-on-demand options

where this could live

these are the platforms that handle the boring stuff (printing, shipping, payment) so a kid with a laptop and an idea can run a clothing brand. i'm researching them. here's the shortlist:

Printful popular, integrates with shopify, etsy, your own site. quality first. printful.com → Printify cheaper than printful, more printer options worldwide. flexible. printify.com → Teespring (Spring) all-in-one: design, sell, ship. has a built-in storefront. teespring.com → Redbubble upload designs, they put them on tshirts/stickers/everything. discovery built-in. redbubble.com → Bonfire campaign-based selling — drop a design, sell for X days, ships once done. bonfire.com → Gelato global print network, eco-focused, well integrated with shopify. gelato.com →
